Overview

Our experienced veterinary team provides a wide range of surgical procedures in a state-of-the-art facility. From routine spay and neuter surgeries to complex soft tissue procedures, your pet is in safe hands. We prioritize pain management and post-operative care to ensure your furry friend recovers comfortably.

What's Included

  • Pre-operative health assessment and blood work
  • General anesthesia with modern monitoring equipment
  • Pain management before, during, and after surgery
  • Spay/Neuter procedures
  • Soft tissue surgery (mass removal, wound repair)
  • Post-operative care instructions and medications
  • Follow-up consultation to monitor recovery

What to Expect

📋 Consultation

We examine your pet and discuss the procedure, risks, and recovery process with you.

💉 Pre-Op

Blood tests ensure your pet is healthy enough for anesthesia. Fasting required before surgery.

🏥 Surgery

Our vet performs the procedure with continuous monitoring of heart rate, oxygen, and temperature.

🏠 Recovery

We monitor your pet until awake, then provide detailed home care instructions and medications.
