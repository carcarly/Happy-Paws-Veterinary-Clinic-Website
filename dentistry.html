Overview

Dental disease is one of the most common health issues in pets. Our professional dentistry services help prevent, diagnose, and treat oral health problems, ensuring your pet maintains healthy teeth and gums for a better quality of life.

What's Included

  • Professional teeth cleaning (scaling and polishing)
  • Dental X-rays to check tooth roots and jawbone
  • Tooth extraction if necessary
  • Treatment of gum disease (periodontal disease)
  • Management of oral infections
  • Home dental care advice

What to Expect

🔍 Examination

Full oral examination under anesthesia to assess teeth and gums.

🧹 Cleaning

Ultrasonic scaling to remove plaque and tartar, followed by polishing.

📷 X-Ray

Digital dental X-rays to detect hidden problems below the gumline.

🏠 Aftercare

Recovery monitoring and personalized home dental care instructions.
