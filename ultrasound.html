Overview

Ultrasound is a non-invasive, pain-free imaging technique that uses sound waves to create real-time images of your pet's internal organs. It's invaluable for diagnosing conditions in the abdomen, heart, and other soft tissues without the need for surgery.

What's Included

  • Abdominal ultrasound
  • Pregnancy diagnosis
  • Heart echocardiography
  • Guided tissue sampling
  • Real-time imaging with immediate results
  • Detailed report and consultation

What to Expect

📋 Prep

Usually requires 12-hour fasting. Water is okay.

🔬 Scan

Your pet lies comfortably while we move the probe over the area.

📊 Results

Images appear on screen in real-time. We'll explain what we see.
