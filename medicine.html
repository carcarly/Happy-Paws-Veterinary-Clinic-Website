Overview

Our medicine services cover all aspects of your pet's health, from routine check-ups to diagnosis and treatment of illness. We believe in preventive care as the foundation of good health, helping your furry companions live longer, happier lives.

What's Included

  • Annual health check-ups and wellness exams
  • Vaccinations and booster shots
  • Parasite prevention and treatment (fleas, ticks, worms)
  • Diagnosis and treatment of common illnesses
  • Prescription medications and supplements
  • Senior pet care and health monitoring
  • Nutritional advice and weight management

What to Expect

📋 Check-Up

Full physical examination from head to tail, checking eyes, ears, teeth, skin, and coat.

💉 Vaccination

Core and non-core vaccines tailored to your pet's lifestyle and risk factors.

🔬 Diagnosis

If your pet is unwell, we perform tests to identify the cause and recommend treatment.

💊 Treatment

Personalized treatment plan including medications, lifestyle changes, and follow-up care.
