Overview

Skin problems are common in pets and can be caused by allergies, parasites, infections, or underlying health conditions. Our dermatology service helps identify the root cause and provides effective treatment to relieve your pet's discomfort.

What's Included

  • Skin allergy testing and treatment
  • Ear infection diagnosis and treatment
  • Parasite detection and prevention
  • Skin cytology and biopsies
  • Hot spot and wound treatment
  • Long-term skin management plans

What to Expect

🔍 Exam

Full skin and ear examination. We check for parasites, infections, and inflammation.

🧪 Tests

Skin scrapings, cytology, or allergy tests may be needed.

💊 Treatment

Personalized plan: medications, shampoos, diet changes, or injections.
